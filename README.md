# worthit.
WorthIt? 🍕🔥 Search a dish, see its calories, and find out how much walking, cycling or squats it takes to burn it off. Finish the challenge to unlock your meal. 
Is that biryani worth it? Find out before you bite.

Features
14 dishes to start with, mostly Indian (dosa, biryani, vada pav, chole bhature and more)
Burn estimates based on standard MET exercise values, adjusted to your weight
Streaks and owed calories saved in your browser’s local storage
Mobile-first, clean UI

Planned
Real food database with portion sizes
Step counter, GPS and Google Fit / Apple Health sync
Camera-based rep counting for squats and pushups (ML Kit / MediaPipe)
Rewards such as coupons and badges
Disclaimer

Calorie and burn numbers are rough estimates for a demo, not medical or nutrition advice.
